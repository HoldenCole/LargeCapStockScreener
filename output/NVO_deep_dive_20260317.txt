27. WHY DID IT SELL OFF? (Multiple compounding factors)
    -------------------------------------------------------
    a) CagriSema REDEFINE 1 Trial Miss (Dec 20, 2024):
       - Phase 3 showed 22.7% weight loss, missing the ~25% expectation
       - Stock dropped ~19% in a single day
       - Raised fears NVO's next-gen obesity drug can't compete with LLY's Zepbound

    b) CagriSema REDEFINE 4 Head-to-Head Loss (Feb 23, 2026):
       - CagriSema achieved 23.0% weight loss vs. tirzepatide's 25.5%
       - Primary endpoint NOT achieved
       - Stock dropped ~16% on the day

    c) 2026 Guidance Cut (Feb 3, 2026):
       - Guided -5% to -13% sales and operating profit decline at CER
       - 8-10% below consensus at midpoint
       - First annual sales decline since 2017
       - Stock fell 18% on Feb 4, touched $35.91 low on March 3

    d) MFN Pricing Deal (Nov 2025):
       - Novo agreed to cut Ozempic/Wegovy prices to ~$350/month from $1,000+
       - Massive immediate revenue per-unit impact

    e) IRA Medicare Negotiation (effective Jan 2027):
       - Ozempic MFP set at $276.67/syringe (71% discount to list)
       - Wegovy MFP at $385.63/4 syringes
       - Medicare spent $14.43B on semaglutide products in 2023-2024

    f) Securities Fraud Investigations:
       - Class Action 1 (Moon v. Novo Nordisk): Alleging CagriSema trial
         design was misrepresented (flexible vs. fixed dosing)
         Class period: Nov 2, 2022 - Dec 19, 2024
       - Class Action 2 (Barta v. Novo Nordisk): Alleging overestimation
         of ability to capture compounded GLP-1 patients
         Class period: May 7, 2025 - July 28, 2025

    g) Repeated 2025 Guidance Cuts:
       - Forecast trimmed 3 times during 2025
       - July 29, 2025: worst single-day drop ~20% on guidance cut

    h) Alzheimer's Trial Failure (Nov 2025):
       - EVOKE and EVOKE+ trials failed to slow cognitive deterioration
       - Stock hit 4-year low ~$45

    i) Market Share Loss to Eli Lilly:
       - NVO's GLP-1 market share fell from ~60% to ~40%
       - LLY now holds ~58-60% of US GLP-1 prescriptions

    j) Leadership Overhaul + Restructuring:
       - ~9,000 jobs cut (11% of workforce)
       - Board overhauled by Novo Nordisk Foundation
       - New CEO Maziar Mike Doustdar

28. ZENAGAMTIDE (AMYCRETIN) PHASE 3 PLANS:
    - Phase 3 AMAZE program initiated Q1 2026 for obesity
    - Testing both injectable (SC weekly) and oral (daily) formulations
    - Phase 2 showed up to 22% weight loss over 36 weeks
    - Diabetes Phase 3 also planned for 2026
    - Dual GLP-1/amylin receptor agonist mechanism
    - Considered NVO's most important next-gen asset after CagriSema
    - UBT251 (triple agonist with Chinese partner) also in development:
      ~20% weight loss in 24-week trial

29. ORAL WEGOVY COMMERCIAL TRACTION:
    - Launched in early 2026 (first GLP-1 pill for obesity)
    - Hit 50,000 weekly prescriptions in under 3 weeks
    - Over 170,000 patients on oral Wegovy within ~4 weeks of launch
    - "More than double the previous branded anti-obesity drugs launched"
    - ~16.6% weight loss efficacy
    - HOWEVER: Goldman Sachs projects LLY's orforglipron will eventually
      dominate the oral GLP-1 segment (60% share vs NVO's 21% by 2030)
    - Key disadvantage: must be taken on empty stomach with 30-min fast
    - LLY's pill can be taken anytime with or without food

30. CAGRISEMA HIGHER-DOSE TRIAL PLANS:
    - CagriSema 2.4 mg/7.2 mg (tripled semaglutide dose) Phase 3 planned
    - Trial initiation: H2 2026
    - Current CagriSema: 2.4 mg cagrilintide + 2.4 mg semaglutide
    - Rationale: higher-dose Wegovy showed ~21% weight loss vs 15-16% standard
    - REDEFINE 11 trial (CagriSema 2.4/2.4 full potential): data H1 2027
    - NDA submitted to FDA Dec 2025 (standard dose); decision expected late 2026

31. MASH PIPELINE (AKERO ACQUISITION) STATUS:
    - Acquired Akero Therapeutics in early 2024 for ~$1.3B
    - Lead asset: Efruxifermin (EFX), an FGF21 analog
    - Was in Phase 2b/3 HARMONY study for MASH with liver fibrosis
    - UPDATE: Novo has wound down early-stage non-metabolic programs including
      liver disease, concentrating resources on core obesity/diabetes
    - Status of EFX specifically in 2026: unclear from available data
    - Note: This consolidation may indicate deprioritization of MASH pipeline

32. SEMAGLUTIDE CKD + HFpEF LABEL EXPANSIONS:
    CKD: APPROVED (Jan 28, 2025)
    - FDA approved Ozempic for reducing risk of worsening kidney disease,
      kidney failure, and CV death in T2D + CKD patients
    - Based on FLOW trial: 24% RRR (4.9% ARR at 3 years)
    - Trial stopped early for efficacy (3,533 patients, 28 countries)
    - First GLP-1 RA approved for CKD indication
    - ~37M adults in US have CKD; 40% of T2D patients develop CKD

    HFpEF: LABEL PARTIALLY EXPANDED
    - SELECT trial: Wegovy approved March 2024 for CV risk reduction
      (heart attack, stroke, CV death) in obese/overweight adults with CVD
    - STEP-HFpEF data: significant improvement in symptoms, physical
      limitations, exercise function in HFpEF + obesity
    - Dedicated HFpEF label: status unclear; likely under regulatory review

    - Debt well-covered by operating cash flow (97.3%)
    - Total debt: DKK 133B; total cash: DKK 26.5B

    - Dividend payout: DKK 51.8B (high but covered by OCF)

35. NVO vs LLY GLP-1 MARKET SHARE:
    - LLY now holds ~58-60% of US GLP-1 market (up from ~47% in early 2025)
    - NVO holds ~40-42% (down from ~53%+ in 2024)
    - LLY overtook NVO in January 2025 and has widened the lead each quarter
    - Combined Mounjaro + Zepbound sales: ~$37B (2025) vs NVO's Ozempic +
      Wegovy ~$32B
    - LLY's drugs considered clinically superior: more weight loss, better
      safety profile
    - Real-world data and REDEFINE 4 head-to-head confirmed LLY's advantage
    - LLY guiding $80-83B revenue for 2026 (growth) vs NVO's -5% to -13%
    - Goldman Sachs projects LLY will also dominate oral GLP-1 segment
    - Medicare coverage of obesity drugs starting July 2026 benefits LLY
      disproportionately (40M new beneficiaries)
